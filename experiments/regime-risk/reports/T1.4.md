- Acceptance on SPY development: B1 beats B0 for `T_down` and `T_volexp`. HAR does not beat trailing 20-day RV on QLIKE. The HAR loss is slightly higher (−2.615 versus −2.637) and the MSE is the same size, so this is not a units bug. HAR was not retuned.
- QLIKE itself is negative. The pre-registered skill `1 − QLIKE_model / QLIKE_HAR` then ranks a worse forecast as positive skill. Week 2 reports the raw QLIKE beside that ratio and does not treat a positive ratio as a better vol forecast.
- QQQ `T_volexp`: B1 is worse than the 3-year base rate. Not retuned. SPY remains the gate symbol.
- Label counts are in `reports/T1.4_label_counts.txt`. SPY 2021 keeps 247 labeled rows because the last five sessions would cross the holdout. Targets stop at 2021-12-31.
- Buy-and-hold has the higher SPY development CAGR. DMA cuts the drawdown and raises turnover to about 7.5 turns a year.
